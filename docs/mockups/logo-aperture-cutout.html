The blade, cut out.

Same body and opening as A. But the blade is now removed from the light rather than added beside it — which is what a real iris does: the blade swings in over the aperture. Every cut is a true fill-rule="evenodd" hole, so it stays transparent on any ground instead of being a black shape painted on top.
